Q. what's minikube?
A. minikube spins up a local Kubernetes cluster on your machine.

Q. On Cloud, what spins up a Kubernetes cluster?
A. On Cloud, you can use services like Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE), Amazon EKS, or Azure AKS to spin up a Kubernetes cluster.

Q. What is kubectl?
A. `kubectl` is the command-line tool for interacting with Kubernetes clusters, allowing you to deploy applications, inspect and manage cluster resources, and view logs.
